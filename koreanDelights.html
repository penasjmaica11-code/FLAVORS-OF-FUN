RAMEN

A popular instant noodle soup with a flavorful broth,
often topped with an egg, vegetables, and meat.
It's a quick and satisfying meal that's enjoyed by people of all ages.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 packs of instant ramen noodles (discard the seasoning packets)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons miso paste (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms (shiitake, button, or any variety you prefer)
  • 2 cups chopped vegetables (bok choy, spinach, carrots, bell peppers, etc.)
  • 2 green onions, chopped (for garnish)
  • Soft-boiled eggs (optional, for topping)
  • Seaweed sheets (nori), thinly sliced (optional, for topping)
  • Sesame seeds (optional, for topping)

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. In a large pot, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and grated ginger. Sauté for about a minute until fragrant.
  2. Pour chicken or vegetable broth and water into the pot. Add soy sauce and miso paste (if using).
    Stir until the miso paste is dissolved into the broth.
  3. Bring the broth to a simmer. Add sliced mushrooms and chopped vegetables.
    Cook for about 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  4. While the broth is simmering, cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions in a separate pot.
    Once cooked, drain and set aside.
  5. Once the vegetables are tender, taste the broth and adjust the seasoning if needed
    (add more soy sauce or miso paste according to your preference).
  6. Divide the cooked ramen noodles into serving bowls.
    Ladle the hot broth and vegetables over the noodles.
  7. Garnish the ramen with chopped green onions, soft-boiled eggs (halved),
    sliced seaweed sheets, and sesame seeds, if desired.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious homemade ramen!

TTEOKBOKKI

Spicy, chewy rice cakes
simmered in a sweet and spicy sauce. It's a popular
street food and a favorite among Koreans.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 200g (7 oz) of cylindrical or oval-shaped rice cakes ("tteok")
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 cups anchovy or vegetable broth (you can also use water)
  • 3 tablespoons gochujang (Korean red chili paste)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon gochugaru (Korean red chili flakes), adjust to taste for spiciness
  • 1 cup sliced fish cakes (optional)
  • 1 cup sliced cabbage or other vegetables (optional)
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, halved (optional)
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • Sesame seeds and/or shredded nori (seaweed), for garnish (optional)

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Soak the rice cakes in cold water for about 30 minutes to soften them. Drain before using.
  2. In a large pan or pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
    Add the sliced fish cakes (if using) and stir-fry for a few minutes until they start to brown.
  3. Add the water and broth to the pan and bring it to a boil.
  4. In a small bowl, mix together the gochujang, soy sauce, sugar, and gochugaru to make the sauce.
  5. Once the broth is boiling, add the sauce to the pan and stir until it's well combined.
  6. Add the soaked rice cakes to the pan and stir to coat them evenly with the sauce.
  7. Reduce the heat to medium-low and let the tteokbokki simmer for about 10-15 minutes,
    stirring occasionally, until the rice cakes are tender and the sauce has thickened.
  8. If using, add the sliced cabbage or other vegetables to the pan and
    cook for a few more minutes until they're tender.
  9. Taste the tteokbokki and adjust the seasoning if needed by adding more sugar,
    soy sauce, or gochugaru according to your preference.
  10. If using, add the halved hard-boiled eggs to the pan and let them warm through in the sauce.
  11. Once everything is cooked through and the sauce has thickened to your liking, remove the pan from the heat.
  12. Serve the tteokbokki hot, garnished with chopped green onions, sesame seeds, and/or shredded nori if desired.

GIMBAP

A rolled seaweed rice dish filled with
various ingredients like vegetables, fish cakes, and meat.
It's a convenient and portable
meal that's perfect for a picnic or lunchbox.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 4 cups cooked short-grain white rice
  • 5-6 sheets of roasted seaweed (gim)
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar (optional)
  • Various fillings (choose from your favorites)

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Prepare the Rice: If you haven't done so already, cook the short-grain white rice according to the package
    Once cooked, transfer the rice to a large bowl.
  2. Prepare the Fillings: Prepare your choice of fillings by cutting them into long, thin strips or slices.
    Make sure they are cooked and seasoned as needed.
  3. Assemble the Gimbap: Lay a bamboo sushi rolling mat (makisu) on a flat surface
    and place a sheet of roasted seaweed (gim) shiny side down on the mat.
  4. Roll the Gimbap: Continue rolling until the entire sheet of seaweed is wrapped around the rice and
    Seal the edge by moistening it slightly with water and pressing it down.
  5. Slice and Serve: Arrange the sliced gimbap on a serving platter and sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds if desired.

CORNDOG

A deep-fried hot dog coated in a crispy corn batter.
It's a popular street food
that's often served with a variety of dipping sauces.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 6 hot dogs or sausages
  • 6 wooden skewers or popsicle sticks
  • 1 cup cornmeal
    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • Optional: shredded cheese for filling
  • Optional: ketchup, mustard, mayonnaise, or other condiments for serving

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Prepare the Hot Dogs: Insert a wooden skewer or popsicle stick into each hot dog, leaving enough space to hold onto the stick.
  2. Prepare the Batter: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
    Be careful not to overmix; a few lumps in the batter are okay.
  3. Coat the Hot Dogs: Dip each hot dog into the batter, making sure it's evenly coated on all sides.
  4. Fry the Corndogs: Fry the corndogs for 3-4 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are golden brown and crispy all over.
  5. Serve the Corndogs: Serve the corndogs hot with your favorite condiments such as ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise.

SAMGYUPSAL

Thinly sliced pork belly grilled on a tabletop grill
and wrapped in lettuce leaves with various side dishes
like kimchi,garlic, and ssamjang sauce. It's a social
dining experience that's enjoyed with friends and family.

    INGREDIENTS

  • 1 pound (450g) pork belly, thinly sliced
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Perilla leaves (optional)
  • Sliced garlic
  • Sliced green onions
  • Kimchi
    • Ssamjang (Korean dipping sauce)
  • Salt
  • Pepper

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Prepare the Grill: If you have a tabletop grill, preheat it to medium-high heat.
    If not, you can use a regular grill pan or skillet on the stovetop.
  2. Season the Pork Belly: Season the pork belly slices with a sprinkle of salt and pepper on both sides.
  3. Grill the Pork Belly: Once the grill is hot, place the pork belly slices on the grill in a single layer.
    Cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, or until they are cooked through and crispy on the edges.
  4. Prepare the Accompaniments: While the pork is cooking, prepare the accompaniments.
    Wash and dry the lettuce and perilla leaves. Slice the garlic and green onions.
    Serve the kimchi and ssamjang in small bowls.
  5. Serve and Enjoy: To eat, place a piece of grilled pork belly onto a lettuce or perilla leaf.
    Add a few slices of garlic, green onions, kimchi, and a dab of ssamjang.
  6. Repeat: Continue grilling and enjoying the pork belly slices with the accompaniments until you've eaten your fill.
